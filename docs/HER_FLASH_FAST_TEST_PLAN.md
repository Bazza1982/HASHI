# HER official DeepSeek Flash fast test plan

## Scope

This campaign is a fast debugging profile. It uses only the official DeepSeek
API and only `deepseek-v4-flash`. OpenRouter, Pro, alternate providers, alternate
models, local models, mocks, and cross-route fallback cannot satisfy a live
cell.

## Ordered effort matrix

Run `fixed` and `flex` at every effort in this order:

1. `low`
2. `medium`
3. `high`
4. `xhigh`
5. `max`
6. `max+`

Each of the 12 CORE-OFF cells retains C00-C09, all eight presentation variants,
native-boundary evidence, three cold exactness repeats, and a warm repeat. Run
the matching 12 HABIT-WIRE cells, two high-effort HABIT-DEEP mode twins, and one
fixed/high HABIT-FAULT packet.

## Fast progression

Run cheap packets before presentation/continuity and expensive boundary,
endurance, and fault work. After a failure, preserve and classify the receipt,
then immediately retry a transient official-route failure or open the defect,
add a regression, repair, rebuild, and exact-retest a product failure.

Do not wait merely for another review or status check. A persisted wait requires
a concrete external blocker: the worker is still active, the official route is
temporarily unavailable and a bounded probe is scheduled, or funds status is
being confirmed. An idle eligible packet dispatches immediately. One stagnant
observation must produce dispatch, a concrete blocker, or a validation finding.

## Required gates

- frozen composite HASHI commit/build, HER source/package, and oracle identity;
- joint offline Layer A;
- exact official provider and Flash model evidence for every live attempt;
- separate CORE-OFF and Habit-on evidence;
- exact failure retest plus affected blast-radius coverage after each repair;
- persona-preserving native-ceiling closeout at all six effort levels in fixed
  and flex: HER supplies the tool-free persona response, HASHI delivers it
  unchanged, and semantic checks confirm progress, uncertainty, stop reason,
  checkpoint/continuation choice, without relying on fixed wording;
- the same native-ceiling persona gate for CORE-OFF and Habit-on; a missing model
  closeout must use the neutral fallback, while plain `max_iterations` defaults
  to `CONTINUE` unless concrete failure or risk evidence supports `PIVOT`;
- affected Flash cells revalidated after a shared-runtime change;
- all 27 live items and ancillary suites green on the final candidate;
- Ajiao restored and all replies drained before closeout.

Only `PASSED` and confirmed official-route `BLOCKED_FUNDS` are terminal.
