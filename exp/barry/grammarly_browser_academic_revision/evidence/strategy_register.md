This register contains de-identified, generalised evidence only. Exact text,
candidates, explanations, URLs, and screenshots remain in confidential AIRMS
run folders.

## Evidence states

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `untested` | A candidate or procedure exists but has no formal outcome. |
| `single-observation` | One academically acceptable target-level observation. |
| `repeated-support` | Independent acceptable support with failures disclosed. |
| `mixed` | Meaningful positive and negative/counterexample evidence. |
| `counterindicated` | Evidence suggests the intervention is usually harmful or ineffective. |
| `retired` | No longer eligible, with history and rationale retained. |

## Operational evidence

### 2026-09-03 — Browser capability

- HASHI Browser Bridge accessed Barry's authenticated Grammarly document.
- Exact marked selections and rendered explanation text were readable.
- One explanation was immediately available on hover.
- Another required `Show explanation`, one bounded retry, and about 15
  seconds.
- This proves a capability, not rewrite efficacy.

### 2026-09-03 — Paper 4 context and batching

- A bounded batch of 8 complete paragraphs and 927 words reconstructed exactly
  in the Grammarly editor.
- R0 recorded 87% and 18 marked parts.
- The ordered 18 severity observations matched the opening subset previously
  reconstructed in the larger body context.
- The larger body and bounded batch had different percentages, confirming that
  percentages are context-dependent and not directly comparable.
- Six R1 candidates were prepared locally, but no safe same-document R1
  reassessment occurred. They contribute zero success or failure counts.

## Update rule

After each validated run:

1. count target-level outcomes, not only percentage movement
2. retain failures and academic-quality rejections beside successes
3. cite run and issue IDs without copying confidential prose
4. state context and detector/UI date
5. disclose simultaneous edits and other causal confounds
6. never promote a tactic from one clearance
